## Play

New Campaigns open a three-step setup for World settings, Player Character, and initial NPCs. Use **Travel** to see direct routes and their durations. You can also describe travel in chat. Route durations are edited in the World tab.

- Use the **God Console** to edit Worlds, Character Profiles, items, quests, location links and NPC routines. Generate AI Drafts and review them before saving.
- The **Journal** records discoveries and quest updates. Quick replies fill the composer so they can be edited before sending.
- **Snapshots** mark a point in a Campaign. Rewind opens a new Branch at that point. Switch back with **Continue** to play the previous timeline.
- **Live Campaign** edits HP, maximum HP, skills and Portraits. Select God's Blessing or Curse for immediate narration, or use **Silent edit**.
- Combat resolves initiative, d20 attacks and damage in code. Set a World’s lethality to control what happens at 0 HP. Advancing time across an hour boundary gives eligible offscreen NPCs one step along their configured route.
- **AI settings** adjusts reasoning effort per tier with Luna in both tiers. **AI inspector** shows recent turns' prompts and tool calls. AI usage shows reported Campaign tokens and an API-equivalent estimate. A Codex subscription is governed by its own usage limits. Portrait generation uses Codex quota. Embeddings have separate API usage.
